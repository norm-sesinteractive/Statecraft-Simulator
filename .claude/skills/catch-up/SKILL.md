# Catch up

1. STATUS.md is already loaded through CLAUDE.md. Also read the newest two or three entries of Docs/WORKLOG.md
   and the current milestone in Docs/ROADMAP.md.
2. Skim the headings of Docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md and the Proposed rows of Docs/decisions/INDEX.md (decisions
   awaiting Norm's sign-off).
3. Use the SessionStart brief (git state, editor, docs drift, lint). If a previous session left drift, list the
   files and their owning docs.
4. Report to Norm in at most 15 lines: the phase; what the last session achieved; loose ends that need Norm;
   the top three next steps with a recommendation; decisions awaiting sign-off.

Do not start work until Norm picks a direction.
