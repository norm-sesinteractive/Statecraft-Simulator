# D-0029: Infrastructure makes trade and military activity efficient, not appealing

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14); revenue rule superseded by D-0030 | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Simulation

## Context
Raised alongside Q-023: what Infrastructure schooling does between nations.

## Decision
Norm: "Infrastructure can represent an increase in efficiency of trade an military activity. Low infrastructure
leads to a incoming and outgoing revenue being reduced by a %, say 50% max and high infrastructure (good) leads to no
reduction in financial comings and goings. Making trade more efficient, but not necessarily more appealing."
- A nation's own infrastructure scales its incoming and outgoing revenue from 50% (none) to 100% (high).
- The same efficiency applies to its military activity [PROPOSED: up to 50% loss of military effectiveness abroad].
- It does not change goodwill or cultural pull.
- [PROPOSED] Infrastructure level comes from the adult cohort's Infrastructure schooling share, reaching "high" at
  a 30% share (an even curriculum gives about 14%), later joined by built infrastructure in the M2 economy. Q-024.

## Because
Norm's call. It separates how well a nation trades and fights from how much others like or imitate it.
