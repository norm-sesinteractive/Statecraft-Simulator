# D-0028: Commerce schooling sets a floor under educational overlap

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14); whose Commerce counts is Proposed | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Simulation

## Context
Q-023: under D-0027, nations with no shared schooling gained no goodwill and no cultural pull from trade.

## Decision
Norm: "Half of a nation's commerce value should be the floor, so they can never get higher than 50% with commerce
alone (no added benefit on top of education matches, just a floor)."
- Effective rate = max(educational overlap, 0.5 x Commerce share). The floor never adds to a match.
- It applies to both the vice change and the goodwill from trade (the two "yields" of D-0027).
- [PROPOSED] The Commerce share that counts is the receiving cohort's own: traders are the ones who learned to deal
  with strangers.

## Because
Norm's call. Commerce becomes the education of dealing with foreigners: it buys a baseline of goodwill and influence
with anyone, capped at half, while true cultural kinship still needs shared schooling.
