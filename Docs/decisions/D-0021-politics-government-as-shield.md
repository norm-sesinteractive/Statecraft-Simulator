# D-0021: The player picks the government type, changes it only by revolution or vote, and uses it as a shield

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Q-013 asked whether the player sets the government form directly or nudges it through ideology and unrest.

## Decision
Norm: "the political system chosen by the player is the government system of the people. If the player wants, they
should be able to change the government type, but not without instigating a revolution of some kind. Democracies
can vote to change to another type, but they can also experience revolts/revolutions. The government is just a
shield for the shadow organization of the player. They are another tool to absorb or stoke the whims of the people
and there to take the fall if the people become unruly."
- Changing type: the player foments a revolution toward a chosen type; a democracy can instead hold a vote. Votes can
  be started by the player or by the people themselves.
- Revolts can also happen on their own when the people become unruly.
- First types: Democracy, Monarchy, Communism, Military junta, Theocracy (unspecified religion). Meritocracy is a
  candidate, held back because it may be overpowered (Q-019).

## Because
Norm's call. It supersedes the [PROPOSED] text in INSTITUTIONS.md that had forms drift without a direct choice.
