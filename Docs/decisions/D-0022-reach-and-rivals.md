# D-0022: Start with one nation, expand by conquest; rival shadow organizations exist

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Q-009 asked about the player's reach and rivals. Cultural conquest (D-0019) means "your population becomes
controlled by some other shadow organization", so rival hands must exist.

## Decision
The player starts with one home nation and can gain more when one of their nations conquers another, militarily
or culturally. Other nations may be steered by rival shadow organizations, which can take the player's nations the
same ways.

## Because
Norm's call ("Start with one, expand"). Symmetry: whatever the player can do to a rival, a rival can do to the player.
