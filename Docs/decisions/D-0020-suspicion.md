# D-0020: Suspicion falls on the government first, then on the shadow organization

**Status:** Accepted in principle (Norm, 2026-10-14); mechanics Proposed | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Norm: "Things like extremely high science and frustrations with infrastructure would increase the likelihood of a
country figuring out something is wrong. There could be many other situations in which one or more of the 7 vices
and or education are out of whack that lead to suspicion of the government and eventually suspicion of you.
Perhaps you foment a false revolution to change government types and try to reduce suspicions."

## Decision
Each nation tracks suspicion in two tiers: suspicion of the government, and suspicion of the shadow organization.
Out-of-balance societies (very high science, infrastructure frustration, vices or schooling far out of balance) raise
government suspicion; government suspicion that is never relieved slowly becomes suspicion of the shadow
organization. A revolution or vote that replaces the government relieves government suspicion (the shield takes the
fall), which is what makes a false revolution a tool. Shadow suspicion past a breaking point risks the overthrow in
D-0019. Proposed details (drivers, dampers, visibility, diminishing returns of repeated false revolutions) are in
Docs/design/HIDDEN_HAND.md.

## Because
Norm's call. Two tiers make the government a real shield (D-0021) and make subtlety pay without limiting the levers.
