# D-0035: Build the economy before trade and war; whole-nation conquest; puppets decide; no rivals yet

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-15) | **Date:** 2026-10-15 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Norm: "the game balance is hard to understand without the cultural and military conquest elements + easier to
understand relations between nations." Before building the world stage, Claude asked four foundational questions.

## Decision
- **Economy first:** "Full M2 economy first": land, resources, population and food (with starvation as a defeat) are
  built before trade, culture flow and war, which then rest on it (trade needs goods and money; D-0030 is money).
- **Military conquest** takes a whole nation: a war fills a war score from relative strength and the winner absorbs
  the loser. Provinces wait for the map.
- **Puppets decide** when to trade and when to go to war, from vices, relations, strength and opportunity. The player
  steers only through the institutions.
- **No rival hands yet:** other nations run on fixed levers with no hand; a nation absorbed by one of them leaves the
  player's control. Rival hands come later.
- The game ends when the player's hand falls in its home nation. What happens when another nation's hand falls is
  open (Q-026).

## Because
Norm's calls. Claude had recommended a minimal-wealth economy alongside trade; Norm chose the full economy first.
