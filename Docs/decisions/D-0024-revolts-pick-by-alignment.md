# D-0024: After a revolt, the people's alignment picks the new government

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Q-020 asked who picks the government type after an emergent revolt.

## Decision
Norm: "A new government post-revolt is selected by the desires of the people. Whatever government type the current
population would be most aligned with, even if it's the same one they just revolted from."
Revolutions the player foments still aim at the player's chosen type (D-0021); revolts the people start pick the
type they are most aligned with.

## Because
Norm's call. Consequence: each government type needs an alignment score computed from the population's culture
(vices, schooling, later ideology). The [PROPOSED] leanings in INSTITUTIONS.md are the first draft of it.
