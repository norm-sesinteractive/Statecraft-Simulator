# D-0023: The hidden-hand and politics proposals are the starting design

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Session 2 left Claude's proposals for government type traits, suspicion drivers and dampers, the leak from
government to shadow suspicion, diminishing false revolutions, suspicion visibility (Q-018), Meritocracy (Q-019)
and the reading of "single nation" versus "expand" (Q-017).

## Decision
Norm: "The proposals are reasonable and acceptable to get the project off the ground." They are the design to build
from, still open to tuning:
- The government type traits table in INSTITUTIONS.md.
- Suspicion drivers, dampers, the leak between tiers and diminishing false revolutions (HIDDEN_HAND.md).
- Suspicion shown as fuzzy gauges plus warning events, never exact numbers (Q-018).
- Meritocracy left out of politics v1 (Q-019).
- Losing the home nation ends the game; nations gained later are setbacks (Q-017).

## Because
Norm's call: concrete enough to build and test, refined by playing.
