# D-0025: Culture flows only through trade imports, from the supplier's adults

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14); "what moves" superseded by D-0027 | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Simulation

## Context
Q-021 asked what cultural conquest means in the simulation.

## Decision
Norm: "Cultural conquests will go along with trade and the interplay between the vices and educations of
neighboring nations."
- Each act of trade nudges the receiving (paying) nation slightly toward the culture (vices and schooling) of the
  supplying nation. Exporting never changes a nation's culture; only receiving does.
- The influence comes from the supplier's adult generation. The receiver's cohorts take it at 2x (youth), 1x
  (adults) and 0.5x (elders).
- Its strength depends on contentment: the supplier's adults' contentment relative to each receiving cohort's own
  contentment ("not against their would-be generational counterparts").
- Cultural conquest grows out of this flow; the exact tipping point is Proposed in WORLD_STAGE.md.

Proposed, needed by the rule above: contentment is computed per cohort, not only per nation (Q-022).

## Because
Norm's call. Culture spreads with goods, and a happy, confident trading partner is persuasive; a miserable one is not.
