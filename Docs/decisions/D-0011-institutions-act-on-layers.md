# D-0011: Each institution acts on its own layer of the river

**Status:** Proposed | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Norm's pitch: media airs "a balance of the 7"; education is powerful over "the outlets/outcomes"; law trades
fear against crime; politics is "semblances of political systems". Q-007.

## Decision
Media acts on vices (mood within months, culture over decades). Education acts on outlets (trained channels that
reach the workforce a generation later). Law and order acts on the overflow (crime, fear, deterrence). Politics
[PLANNED] acts on steering (who decides, legitimacy, how grievance is voiced).

## Because
It keeps every lever distinct and gives each its own time horizon, which turns timing into the strategy. It also
follows the pitch's own wording. The alternative, every institution nudging every vice, makes the levers
interchangeable.
