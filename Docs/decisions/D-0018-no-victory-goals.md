# D-0018: No victory goals; the player plays for their own designs

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Q-001 asked what the hidden hand is trying to achieve (sandbox, scenario objectives, agenda score).

## Decision
Norm: "The shadow organization has no game goals, it's simply driven by the desire of the player to manipulate the
population(s) of the game." There is no win condition or score. The game ends only in defeat (D-0019).

## Because
Norm's call. Consequence: the game must make manipulation itself interesting to watch and to read (legible
causality, D-0013), and defeat threats (D-0019, D-0020) supply the tension that goals would otherwise supply.
