# D-0019: Four ways to lose a nation; losing the home nation ends the game

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14); the home-nation reading is Proposed | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Design

## Context
Q-002 asked what limits the levers. Norm: "Nothing limits the levers persay, but the available countries should be
able to be defeated." Follow-ups: "Single nation, loss ends game" and "Start with one, expand."

## Decision
Nothing limits the levers. A nation is lost to the player when:
1. it is conquered militarily by another country;
2. it is culturally conquered: "your population becomes controlled by some other shadow organization";
3. its people run out of resources and starve;
4. a revolt overthrows the shadow organization itself (a revolt that only topples the government is survivable).

Proposed reading of the two follow-up answers: the player starts with one home nation and may gain others by
conquering them militarily or culturally. Losing the home nation ends the game; losing a gained nation is a setback.
Open as Q-017 until Norm confirms.

## Because
Norm's call: risk replaces limits. Free levers keep controls simple; the danger lies in what extreme settings do.
