# D-0031: The infrastructure score is the adult generation's Infrastructure schooling

**Status:** Accepted (Norm, 2026-10-14) | **Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Area:** Simulation

## Context
Q-024 asked where D-0030's 0 to 100 infrastructure score comes from. Claude had suggested scaling a 30% share to 100.

## Decision
Norm: "Infrastructure score comes from the education of the active/adult generation. No to the 30%."
Infrastructure score = the adult cohort's Infrastructure schooling share x 100, with no rescaling. Efficiency =
50% at 0, 100% at 100, linear (D-0030).

## Because
Norm's call. Consequence: efficient trade takes a real commitment of the schools. An even curriculum (about 14%
Infrastructure) gives about 57% efficiency, so a trading nation must choose between efficient logistics and the
other focuses, and the payoff arrives a generation after the schools change.
